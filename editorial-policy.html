Who publishes the research.

Stratum Wealth publishes Stratum Intelligence, its market intelligence newsletter for retail investors.

Publisher and contact

Stratum Wealth is based in Ontario, Canada. Product questions, factual corrections and questions about a report go to stratumwealth@stratumwealth.ca. Include the edition date and the statement you are asking about.

AI and review

Stratum uses AI to help collect and synthesize public information and produce research briefings. Automated checks form part of that process. We do not claim that every report is personally reviewed by a credentialed financial analyst. AI and automated checks can miss errors.

Research should distinguish what a source reports from our interpretation of it. A conviction label, score or stock verdict is a research assessment, not a measured probability of profit. Readers should check primary sources and current conditions before acting.

Corrections

When a material error is confirmed in public research, the corrected page should identify what changed and its correction date. Historical views should retain their original dates. A correction must not be presented as an original successful prediction.

Subscriber reports may contain views that later change. A changed view is different from a factual correction; both should be explained in context.

Conflicts and paid relationships

Paid placements and affiliate links must be identified where they appear. Material financial interests relevant to a published research item should be disclosed alongside that item. Sponsorship must not be presented as independent research.

This policy is not a declaration that the publisher or contributors hold no investments. For questions about an interest relevant to a specific report, contact Stratum before relying on it.

Scope and limitations

Stratum publishes informational and educational research. It does not provide personalized portfolio management, tax or estate planning. Market views can be wrong. A selected example is not a complete track record, and past outcomes do not establish future results.
