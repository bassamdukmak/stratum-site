About Stratum Wealth.

Stratum Wealth publishes Stratum Intelligence, an AI-assisted market intelligence newsletter for retail investors.

What we publish

Our reports bring together global market context, ranked stock research and dated research views. Free subscribers receive one full report each week. Plus and Quant include weekday briefings before North American market open. Plus adds the FA Deep Dive; Quant also includes weekly and monthly reports and Congressional Trading Watch. Current plan details are on the homepage.

How to evaluate the research

Start with the historical report excerpt and Daily reading guide to understand the format, conviction labels, horizons and exits. Our public research includes dated analysis with sources and limitations. Selected historical examples are not a complete track record and do not establish future results.

Publisher and accountability

Stratum Wealth is based in Ontario, Canada. AI helps collect and synthesize public information; automated checks can still miss errors. We do not claim that every edition is personally reviewed by a credentialed financial analyst. Read our editorial standards and corrections policy for how facts, interpretations, corrections and conflicts should be handled.

Scope and contact

Stratum publishes informational and educational research. It does not manage portfolios or provide personalized investment, tax or estate planning. Product questions, report corrections and privacy requests can be sent to stratumwealth@stratumwealth.ca. See contact details, Terms and our Privacy Policy.
