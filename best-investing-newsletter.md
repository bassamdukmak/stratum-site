## Common Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free tier costs $0 forever with no credit card required and includes one full report each week. Plus ($19.99/mo) adds weekday delivery and the FA Deep Dive. Quant ($49.99/mo) also includes weekly summaries, monthly wrap-ups, and full Congressional Trading Watch.

How is regional coverage selected?

Stratum follows material signals wherever they originate. The mix can change across languages and markets from one report to the next.

What time does it arrive?

Plus and Quant reports arrive every weekday before North American markets open. Free subscribers receive one report each week in the same before-open window. The exact release time is shown on each report.

What is Congressional Trading Watch?

US lawmakers must disclose stock trades under the STOCK Act. Stratum reports on these public disclosures and notes timing patterns relative to public events such as committee hearings or policy votes. We report on public information only and do not allege or imply illegal activity by any individual. Available on the Quant tier.
