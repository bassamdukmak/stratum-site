Start here.

You don’t need to follow every stock or understand every market headline. Here’s how to get your first report, find the parts that matter to you, and make sense of what you’re reading.

First, make sure we can reach you.

Signed up for Free or a trial? Open the verification email and confirm your address. Your seven-day Plus trial starts when you verify. If you bought a paid plan, keep your checkout receipt and use the same email when managing your subscription.

Check Spam, Promotions and any other inbox tabs if you can’t find a Stratum email. Mark it as wanted mail and add the sender to your contacts.

A seven-day trial doesn’t mean seven market-day reports. Weekends fall inside the trial period. At the end of the no-card trial, you continue on Free unless you choose a paid plan.

Your first read: don’t start with every ticker.

Start with the market overview. Get the context for the edition before looking at individual ideas.

Then choose one idea. Pick a company or topic you already recognize. Read the explanation, the stated research view and any risks alongside it. You can skip the rest and come back later.

Save the email. The report date gives you a reference point when you revisit a view. Reading an idea does not create an obligation to trade it.

What am I looking at?

In your reportIn plain English
Stock verdictThe report’s research view on a company. Read it with the explanation, not on its own.
Conviction scoreA score used in the research presentation. It is not your chance of making money.
Regional RadarMarket context from different regions. The countries and topics can change between editions.
FA Deep DiveA closer look at a covered company, included with Plus and Quant. “FA” does not mean you have a personal financial advisor.
Congressional Trading WatchPublicly disclosed US congressional trades. A filing may appear after the trade; it does not establish current holdings or wrongdoing.

A “Buy” label is not a command.

A stock can be interesting and still be wrong for your circumstances. A verdict doesn’t account for your portfolio, cash needs or tolerance for losses. Stratum is informational research, not personalized investment advice.

“Wait” and “Avoid” are useful conclusions too. You do not need to take action after every report.

By the end of your first week.

You should know whether the cadence fits your routine, which sections you use, and whether the briefings help you understand the market. Evaluate that before judging the subscription by a single stock’s short-term move.

Past winning examples on the website are selected examples, not a complete track record. No report promises a return.

Your subscription stays in your hands.

Use Manage subscription on the website and enter your subscription email. We’ll send a secure billing-portal link where you can change or cancel a paid plan.

To stop newsletter emails, use the unsubscribe link in an email. For a paid plan, use the billing portal to check or cancel your subscription rather than relying on an email unsubscribe.

For a billing question or something unclear in a report, contact Stratum. Include the report date if it’s a research question. Never send your password or card details.
